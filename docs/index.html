Who we are

Malaysia's community for data management

We are DAMA Chapter Malaysia – Kuala Lumpur & Selangor, officially approved by DAMA International on 1 March 2023 and launched in May 2025.

Our mission is to promote and disseminate the culture and knowledge of data management across Malaysia's institutions (academia, private sector and public sector) to improve the quality of decision-making and contribute to the improvement of society.

  • To be the go-to data community in Malaysia for resources, education and knowledge exchange
  • To be the world leader in best practices in data management
  • To nurture data professionals who champion the value of data management

What we do

Our goals

Empowering Malaysia's data management community through education, networking and professional development.

01

World-Class Practices & Standards

We provide and promote world-class data management practices and standards through conferences, workshops and special interest groups.

02

Data Value Awareness

We raise awareness of data as a fundamental asset to any organisation, helping to institutionalise a pervasive culture of data management.

03

Community & Networking

We build an open and trusted community where local data practitioners and enthusiasts share information, problems, ideas, experiences and resources.

04

Professional Development

We offer development and recognition through education and training programmes that support Certified Data Management Professional (CDMP) certification.

05

National Digital Economy

We contribute to Malaysia's digital economy by spreading sound data management practices for inclusive, responsible and sustainable socioeconomic development.

Join DAMA Chapter Malaysia and invest in your data management career with flexible membership options.

Why join

Benefits of membership

Become part of Malaysia's premier data management community and accelerate your professional growth.

Learn

Access up-to-date industry knowledge and insights from data management experts. Advance your career with CDMP certification support and exclusive access to DAMA Body of Knowledge (DMBOK) resources and learning materials.

Contribute

Influence local and global data management standards and best practices. Share your expertise to gain recognition among peers and industry leaders, and shape the future of data management in Malaysia.

Network

Connect with data professionals locally and globally. Attend exclusive events including conferences, workshops and summits, and build valuable affiliations with tech providers and consulting communities.

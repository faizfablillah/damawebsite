MoU Signing with MMU

DAMA Kuala Lumpur & Selangor signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Multimedia University (MMU), strengthening ties between our data community and academia.

MoU Signing with AFED Digital

Partnership

MoU Signing with Universiti Malaya

21 August 2025 Universiti Malaya, Kuala Lumpur

Universiti Malaya, CADS and DAMA Kuala Lumpur & Selangor signed a Memorandum of Understanding to partner on AI and data literacy.

Official Launch of DAMA Malaysia

13 May 2025 Majestic Hotel, Kuala Lumpur

DAMA Malaysia – Kuala Lumpur & Selangor was officially launched at the PwC AI Leadership Conference 2025. The DAMA track included an introduction to the chapter, the launch ceremony and a panel discussion.
